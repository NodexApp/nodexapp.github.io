Nodex Mobile

iOS · Android

TestFlightDownload APK

Manage your computer’s chats and Inbox from your phone, over your local network or Tailscale.

  1. Keep Nodex open on your computer and turn on Remote access in Settings → Spaces.
  2. Choose Link a device and scan the code with the app.

Every Nodex release updates the phone app too. TestFlight keeps it current on iPhone; on Android, install the newer APK over the old one and your linked Spaces stay.

Nightly builds and every earlier release are on GitHub Releases.
