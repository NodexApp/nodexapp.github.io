Privacy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What this site collects

The Nodex website counts page views and clicks on download buttons, using Statsig. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, so one visit cannot be connected to another. It does not record what you read, and it stops counting when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. There is no advertising and no account creation.

Download and outbound links

When you click download or repository links, you leave this site and go to third-party services such as GitHub, Homebrew and Apple's TestFlight. Those services may collect network logs, browser metadata, and any information you choose to provide there.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which keep their own network logs. Nodex reads daily totals of update-check requests from Cloudflare, not individual visitors.

Usage analytics in the desktop app

The app sends anonymous usage analytics to Statsig so we can tell how many people use Nodex and which parts work. It is on by default, says so on first launch, and sends nothing until you have had time to turn it off. Turn it off any time in Settings → General → Privacy, or set DO_NOT_TRACK=1. Turning it off discards anything not yet sent.

Each event carries a random ID that is created on your computer and is not connected to your account, plus the app version, release channel, operating system, processor type and language. Events say that something happened: a launch, a conversation started, which Agent and public model it used, how a turn ended and how many tokens it used, how long it took. Prompts, responses, code, titles, file names and paths are never sent, and a model you configured privately is reported only as “other”. Statsig receives your IP address as any web service does and uses it to derive a country. Settings shows exactly what has been sent.

Crash reports in the desktop app

Crash reports go to Sentry so we can fix failures. They are on by default and are scrubbed on your computer first: prompts, transcripts, card text, local paths, credentials and request bodies are removed. Turn them off in Settings → General → Privacy. Session Replay is a separate setting that stays off unless you enable it, and it masks all text and inputs when you do.

Nodex Mobile

Nodex Mobile connects directly to Nodex on your computer over your local network or Tailscale, encrypted end to end, with no server of ours in between. It sends no usage analytics or crash reports. Your phone keeps its device credentials, messages not yet delivered, drafts and a copy of what it last showed you.

On iPhone, if you turn notifications on, the computer running that Space encrypts each one for your phone before sending it. Our relay and Apple deliver it without being able to read it. They receive your device's notification token to route it, and the relay stores neither the token nor the notification.

Your content

Nodex stores your Pages and conversations on your computer. Analytics and crash reports do not include them. Agents you use send your messages to the provider you chose, under that provider's terms.

Contact and updates

If the privacy posture of the site changes, this page will be updated alongside the source in the public repository.
